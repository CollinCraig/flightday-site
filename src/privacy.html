Privacy

Your house stays yours

Flight Day collects no data. No accounts, no analytics or telemetry, no tracking and no Flight Day cloud. Nothing is sent to us, so there’s nothing to sell. This page lists what each part does talk to, and why.

Effective 14 November 2026 · Questions: hello@flightday.app

The short version

  • No data collected. No accounts, no analytics or telemetry, no crash reporting, advertising or tracking code in any Flight Day app, and no Flight Day cloud. The Apple apps declare no tracking and no collected data.
  • Your data stays in your house: in your Home Assistant, on the optional Flight Day Hub on your own computer, and on each screen.
  • Public services get only what they need: launch schedules, the ISS position and NASA pictures need nothing personal. Weather services get your home’s location.
  • Apple Health is opt-in, read-only and stays home. See Apple Health.
  • This website has no cookies, no analytics and loads nothing from anyone else.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

On iPhone, Flight Day uses HealthKit to read your sleep from Apple Health, to show last night in the briefing and the night view. It’s off until you turn it on.

  • Read only, sleep only. It reads HealthKit Sleep Analysis for the last 8 days. It never writes anything to Apple Health.
  • It stays on your iPhone. Your iPhone works out nightly summaries. Raw Health samples never leave the phone.
  • Sharing is a second switch. If you turn it on, the summary goes only to your own Flight Day Hub: a random id, a name you choose, last night’s bed and wake times, minutes asleep, in bed and awake, sleep stages, a 7-day average and the number of nights.
  • On the hub, reading it needs the hub’s key. It expires after 48 hours, is deleted when you turn sharing off, and is never logged.
  • Who sees it: anyone looking at a house screen when it shows last night.
  • It never goes to us, and it leaves your home network only if you reach your hub from outside yourself.

Permissions

The Apple apps ask for each permission only when a feature first needs it: Calendar, Notifications (for the alarm), Local Network (to find Home Assistant) and HealthKit (sleep, read-only).

  • Calendar: the apps read your device calendar (the next 7 days) for Today and the briefing. They write to it only when you add an event yourself. Everything stays on your device and in your own calendar account; nothing is sent to us.
  • Health (HealthKit): read access to sleep only. No permission to write to Health is requested. See Apple Health.
  • Camera: none. “Live view” means watching your own home cameras through Home Assistant.
  • Notifications: local only, for the alarm. No push service.
  • Location: none. Your home’s location comes from Home Assistant or what you type in.
  • Local network: to find your Home Assistant.
  • Android accessibility service: it sees only which app is in front, so the TV can return to Flight Day. It can’t read what’s on screen.

AI features

Flight Day has no AI service and no AI keys of its own. When released, AI features go through the AI provider you choose in your Home Assistant, and what they send is listed with each feature. In the one household that runs Flight Day today, the AI line in the briefing sends only public headlines, the Systems check (in development) sends device findings with names and addresses removed, and the AI comfort plan (in development) sends room readings, temperatures, wake times and calendar busy times without titles.

Apple Weather

When a forecast or the next-hour rain line comes from Apple’s WeatherKit, the screen shows the Apple Weather mark and a link to Apple’s legal attribution for weather data, wherever that data appears. WeatherKit is used only through your own Home Assistant, or through the hub with your own WeatherKit key.

Worth knowing

  • Weather needs your location. Open-Meteo, the National Weather Service and, if you set it up, Apple WeatherKit get your home’s coordinates. The wall and TV app sends them unrounded today; rounding them is planned.
  • The Rain sleep sound streams from a public internet radio station. A built-in sound is planned.
  • YouTube may set cookies when Live ambient or Watch plays a stream.
  • On your home network, the hub’s space data and screen settings can be read without a key. Only sleep summaries need one.
  • AccuWeather and Apple Weather through Home Assistant are fetched by Home Assistant, not by Flight Day. Flight Day only reads what your Home Assistant already has.

This website

flightday.app loads only its own files: no third-party scripts, fonts, analytics or cookies, enforced by its content security policy. The only thing it stores is your light or dark theme choice, in your own browser. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which keeps standard request logs.
