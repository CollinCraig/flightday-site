Roadmap

Private build, in flight, next

Nothing here is marked done that isn’t. “In flight” means someone is building it now. “Next” is planned, without a date.

Fully free at launch. In development, aiming for 8 Dec: web/Android and iPhone first. Apple TV, Mac and Watch are coming after launch.

As of 16 November 2026 · Mission Log: what’s new on this site

Private build

  • Launch alarm and briefing
    Countdown, liftoff on time, a warm light ramp and the briefing until Done or two hours.
  • Smart snooze
    Snooze, I’m up, Scrub, −1 min, Launch now, Back to bed and a “must be up by” limit.
  • Sleep schedule
    Mon–Fri, Saturday and Sunday groups with bedtime targets. 12-hour time by default.
  • Goodnight and the night screen
    Sleep sound with live speaker volume, dark screens and a dim red night overlay.
  • Thermostat and comfort schedule
    Night, Wake and Day temperatures. Off is always respected.
  • Smart Comfort controls
    Auto, Heat, Cool and Off, fresh air and holds with Resume, on every screen.
  • Weather
    Apple Weather, optional AccuWeather and Open-Meteo as the free backup, with rain soon on every screen.
  • Space
    Launch manifest, globe, ISS passes, visible launches and Flight Director.
  • House
    Lights over Matter, scenes, media, camera stills and the robot vacuum.
  • iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch in private testing
    Private TestFlight builds, with alarm buttons on every device, Lock Screen widgets and offline “as of” mode.
  • Open source
    The code is open source: github.com/CollinCraig/flightday.

In flight

  • Smart Comfort automatic mode
    Watching in shadow, with an audit of its decisions before it’s allowed to act. Manual Off still wins.
  • AI comfort plan
    A plan made from the forecast and the rooms, checked by rules before anything acts. It runs alongside shadow mode.
  • Apple feature parity
    Tomorrow-only wake change, the same schedule and comfort settings, time format, and weather and Today on Apple TV Home.
  • Polish on the wall and TVs
    The backlog of small fixes and the last gaps in the feature checklist.
  • Flight Day for everyone
    Install with Home Assistant, pair screens from your phone, Settings › Integrations and the public extension API. Official launch planned for 8 Dec.
  • The new name
    Flight Day is rolling out to every app, icon and launch screen.
  • This site
    Live at flightday.app, now with docs and a privacy page. Next: a preview of the full vision, clearly labelled.

Next

  • Room readings
    Temperature and humidity from smart speakers in each room, through one home automation.
  • Go / No-Go briefing In development (in the demo house; arrives with the next app release)
    One line at the top of the morning, for example “Checks incomplete · weather as of 6:10 AM”.
  • Debrief
    An evening look back: what got done, tomorrow’s first event and the wake time.
  • AI help around the house
    A “what needs me” check, launch viewing plans, a weekly comfort note, Ask the house and a spoken morning summary.
  • Flight Plan
    An AI-made plan for the day from your calendar and routines.
  • Door sensor for fresh air
    Optional, so fresh-air mode knows when a window or door is open.

Not promised

What we’re not saying

There’s no App Store release date; iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch are in private TestFlight testing; Mac and Apple TV are in development. Building in public. Early access soon, official launch planned for 8 Dec. The install guide is being prepared; for now there’s no installer for other homes, and Flight Day runs on one household’s devices with hands-on setup. No setup time is promised until it’s been measured. A video doorbell integration is on hold.
