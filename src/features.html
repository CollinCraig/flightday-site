Part one

The alarm clock

The front door, and the part everything else builds on. Every screen counts down to your wake time, then hands you the day. At night, one tap puts the house to bed.

House rules

What every build follows

Nothing on the wall is fake

A tile shows real data or a standby that says what it’s waiting for. Sample content appears only in demo mode, and it’s labelled.

Night is quiet

Red and amber on black, no brand colours, nothing visible or audible on bedroom screens at night unless you ask.

Warm light only

No routine sets white light.

Readable and calm

Text meets 4.5:1 contrast (the deliberately dim night clock aside) and Reduce Motion is respected everywhere.
