Requirements and setup

An overview of what Flight Day runs on today. The install guide is being prepared.

Not available to install yet. Flight Day runs in one household today, set up by hand. The iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch apps are in private TestFlight testing; Mac and Apple TV are in development. There is no installer for other homes yet; it’s in development.

What runs it today

  • Home Assistant keeps the wake schedule, the alarm and the house in one place, and keeps every screen in step.
  • Screens: an Android wall panel, Google TVs, with iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch in private TestFlight testing. Mac and Apple TV apps are in development. The alarm sound plays on a screen; on your chosen alarm screen.
  • Flight Day Hub (optional), on a computer in the home. It shares one space-data cache, the next-hour rain forecast, screen settings, Track and sleep summaries between screens. Without it, each screen uses the public feeds directly.

Optional, when you have them

  • A bedroom speaker, smart lights, a thermostat, a robot vacuum, cameras and room sensors, through Home Assistant
  • AccuWeather or Apple Weather in Home Assistant (Open-Meteo is built in)
  • Calendars, and a usage report for plan limits

Privacy

No accounts, no Flight Day cloud and no tracking. Your data stays in your house. See Privacy.
