What you can add

Fully free at launch. In development, aiming for 8 Dec: web/Android and iPhone first. Apple TV, Mac and Watch are coming after launch. These docs also describe later platform work.

The alarm clock is the front door. Add what you have, and Flight Day grows into your whole home dashboard and day planner.

Each addition is optional because you add it, not because it’s minor. It appears when the device or service is there, and nothing complains when it isn’t.

How it’s set up today

Today Flight Day runs with Home Assistant, which keeps the schedule and the house in one place and every screen in step. The integrations above come through it. See Requirements and setup.

Solo flight: works without Home Assistant on one device, with the alarm, your calendar, weather and Space. In development
