Smart Comfort and the thermostat

Your thermostat, with a schedule that follows your night and morning. It lights up when your thermostat is in Home Assistant. Off always stays off until you say otherwise.

Heat, Cool, Off and Auto

  • House › Thermostat has Auto, Heat, Cool and Off, plus − and +.
  • Auto hands the thermostat back to Smart Comfort. It isn’t a thermostat mode.
  • With the thermostat Off, the setpoint shows “—” and − and + are locked: “Choose Heat or Cool to see its setpoint”.

Manual hold and Resume

Pressing − or + starts a manual hold (“Setpoint · manual hold”). It lasts until the next period, or 1, 2 or 4 hours, as set in Manual changes last. Resume ends a hold early. A manual Off stays off until you choose Resume Auto.

The comfort schedule

Three temperatures: Night when you choose Goodnight, Wake ten minutes before your wake time, and Day from the time Day starts. Edit them in House › Thermostat, Night › Tonight or Settings. The schedule only changes the setpoint; it never turns the system on or off.

Fresh air

When the weather outside is right, Flight Day can suggest opening up. Fresh air mode starts it, Close up · Auto ends it and hands back to Auto, and Keep open 1 h extends it. You can also dismiss a suggestion: Not today, Just off, Keep off or Leave it.

Automatic mode

Smart Comfort’s automatic mode is In development. While it learns, it watches only: “Auto is watching only. Manual Heat, Cool and Off still work.” Ending the watch never clears a manual Off.

Room readings

In development Each room shows its temperature and humidity with its age (“3 min ago”) or “Waiting for a reading”. Old readings are dimmed and marked “not counted”. Until readings arrive, the card says “Room sensors · waiting for Apple Home”.

The AI plan

In development An AI-made plan for the day’s temperatures, checked by the house rules before anything acts. It shows “Plan · …” with Why and Skip today. A plan shown while the system is manually Off hasn’t been applied.

At night, bedroom screens say “Comfort is quiet for the night”.
