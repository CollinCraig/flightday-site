The sleep schedule

Fully free at launch. In development, aiming for 8 Dec: web/Android and iPhone first. Apple TV, Mac and Watch are coming after launch. These docs also describe later platform work.

A weekly plan for waking up, a separate change for tomorrow only, and a bedtime target that never starts anything by itself.

The weekly plan

  1. Open Night › Schedule, or Settings › Night & alarm › Schedule.
  2. Each group, Mon–Fri, Saturday and Sunday, has a wake time, alarm on or off and a bedtime target, shown as “bed … the night before · wake …”.
  3. Turn Customize days on to set weekdays one by one. Days that differ split out by themselves.
  4. Use −15 / +15 or the hour and minute editor, then Apply or Cancel.

Tomorrow only

For a one-off morning, use Tomorrow only in Night. It shows “Tomorrow: … (just once)” and leaves the weekly plan alone. Use weekly plan removes it. Changing the wake time from the night controls is also tomorrow-only.

Bedtime targets

Monday’s bedtime is Sunday night’s target before Monday’s wake. A target helps you see how much sleep is left. It never starts Goodnight, changes a light, starts a sound, touches the thermostat or turns a screen off.

Who’s launching and up by

The crew is everyone who lives in the home; each of them is a crew member.

  • Who’s launching per day: the whole crew gets the full bedroom alarm; one crew member gets a quiet alarm on their own phone.
  • Up by per day sets the latest you can snooze to. Leave it on Automatic to use your calendar and the snooze window. See Snooze and “must be up by”.

Last night and recent mornings

When a real source exists, Night shows last night (“Goodnight … · target …”) and the last seven mornings. A night recorded before targets were saved says “target not recorded” instead of borrowing today’s.

Times follow the shared 12-hour or 24-hour setting. Changing the format never moves the scheduled moment.
