Lights, speakers and cameras

House shows the lights, scenes, speakers, TVs and cameras your Home Assistant has, and what each one reports. Each appears only when it’s there.

OptionalIn development

Lights and scenes

  • Each light has a toggle and a level. All on and All off cover the lot.
  • Scenes run your Home Assistant scenes and scripts.
  • Turning a light on asks for its warmest colour. Nothing in Flight Day sets white light, and the alarm raises brightness, never colour temperature.

Speakers and what’s playing

Now playing shows the title, transport and volume for the selected player. Speakers show their state and can be grouped. A bedroom speaker can play the sleep sound from Goodnight, and the alarm raises it to the volume limit you set (Settings › Night & alarm).

TVs

TVs show their state, input and volume. On a Google TV that runs Flight Day, the Home card has Back to Apple TV, and a Sony with Picture off set up goes dark at night while it keeps playing the sleep sound.

Cameras

Each camera shows the still from its last event. Tap for live view on Apple devices. The wall panel and TVs don’t promise live video from a source that hasn’t been checked.
