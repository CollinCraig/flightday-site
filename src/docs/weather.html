Weather and rain soon

The forecast, the next hour of rain and any weather alert, each with its source named.

Where the forecast comes from

Open-Meteo is built in: free, with no key. If your Home Assistant has AccuWeather or Apple Weather, Flight Day prefers fresh data from those, section by section, and falls back to Open-Meteo for anything missing. The credit line names the mix, for example “AccuWeather · hourly: Open-Meteo”. RealFeel appears only with a real AccuWeather value; otherwise it says “Feels like”.

Weather needs a home location. It comes from Home Assistant, or you can type it in Settings › Home location. Without one, no weather is shown.

Rain soon

A one-line rain forecast for the next hour, for example “Rain starting ~8:10 AM · light” or “Rain for the next 30 min, ending ~9:40 AM”. A dry forecast shows no line, on purpose. A hint that stops updating disappears after 30 minutes.

It uses Apple’s next-hour forecast when the hub has one, and Open-Meteo otherwise. When Apple data is shown, its attribution and legal link are shown too.

Quiet at night

Bedroom screens hide rain hints at night. Living-room screens keep them.

Open in AccuWeather

Opens the AccuWeather app if it’s installed, or a web page with Close and Back. It doesn’t change where the dashboard gets its data. Not on Apple TV.

Units

°F or °C, in Settings › Home location on the wall panel and TVs.
