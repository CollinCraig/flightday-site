Words we use

Fully free at launch. In development, aiming for 8 Dec: web/Android and iPhone first. Apple TV, Mac and Watch are coming after launch. These docs also describe later platform work.

Flight Day borrows a few words from spaceflight. Each one has a plain meaning, and the app shows that meaning next to it wherever it isn’t obvious.

In the apps today

WordWhat it means
LiftoffThe moment your alarm goes off: your wake time. See The launch alarm.
CountdownT-minus · T-plusThe ten minutes before liftoff while the screen brightens. T-minus is the time left; T-plus is the time since.
SnoozeTen more minutes. The screen shows the new liftoff, for example “Snoozed · liftoff 7:57 AM”.
I’m upYou’re awake. The alarm stops and the morning briefing follows.
ScrubAlarm off todayTurns today’s alarm off. It’s a deliberate hold, so a sleepy tap can’t cancel the morning.
Launch nowStarts a ten-second final count, rather than skipping straight to liftoff.
Back to bedReturns only the bedroom to night, until noon.
GoodnightOne tap dims the screens for the night. See Goodnight and the night screen.
BriefingThe morning summary after liftoff: weather, rain soon, your day and the next launch. See The morning briefing.
Flight DirectorThe screen that takes over near a featured launch, before its stream. See The Space feed.
CrewPeople who live hereThe people who live in the home; each of them is a crew member. Who’s launching picks which of the crew the alarm wakes each day. See The sleep schedule.
LabsFeatures you can try before they’re finished, off until you turn them on, such as Smart Comfort’s automatic heat and cool.

Arriving with the next app releases

These names are decided and arrive with the next app releases. Some of the features they name aren’t built yet, and say so.

WordWhat it means
Systems checkWhat needs attention at homeBatteries, offline devices, automation errors: anything at home that needs a hand. In development See Systems check.
Caution · WarningHow serious a Systems check finding is. Caution needs attention; Warning is a problem. When there’s nothing, it says “Nothing needs you”.
Flight LogYour last 7 morningsWhen you got up each day, next to when the alarm was set. Today’s heading is Sleep, or Last 7 mornings.
Cabin climateSmart Comfort or ThermostatThe heading of the thermostat and comfort card in the House area.
CommsNotices from Flight Day and your integrationsThe list of notices. Each notice itself stays plain.
Pre-launch checklistStep 3 of 7The progress line above each setup step. In development
Solo flightThis device only, no Home AssistantWorks without Home Assistant on one device, with the alarm, your calendar, weather and Space. In development
Mission LogWhat’s new in each releaseThe What’s new page. This site’s is the Mission Log; in the apps, the Settings row still says What’s new.
Go / No-GoThe morning verdict at the top of the briefing, with its reason, for example “Go. Dry until 6:00 PM”. It means nothing else in Flight Day. In development (in the demo house; arrives with the next app release)
DebriefAn evening look back: what got done, tomorrow’s first event and the wake time. In development
Flight PlanOnly ever the AI-made plan for your day. Your calendar stays Today and Tomorrow. Concept

What always stays plain

  • Settings rows, unless the plain meaning is on the same row.
  • Permission prompts and system dialogs.
  • Errors, and the labels that tell you what’s real: Real, Sample data, Concept, AI-made and “as of”.
  • The Night area and the night screen: labels and values only.
  • VoiceOver and TalkBack, which always read the plain meaning, for example “Turn off today’s alarm” for Scrub.
