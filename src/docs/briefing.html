The morning briefing

Fully free at launch. In development, aiming for 8 Dec: web/Android and iPhone first. Apple TV, Mac and Watch are coming after launch. These docs also describe later platform work.

At liftoff the countdown hands over to the day: weather, your calendar, what’s due and the news, in numbers rather than adjectives.

What’s in it

  • The header: a greeting, the time and date, and the rain hint for the next hour.
  • Weather: the temperature and summary, “Feels like” (or RealFeel with AccuWeather), sunrise and sunset, any weather alert and a 12-hour strip. Each part names its source.
  • Your day: Today or Tomorrow with countdowns, and what’s due today and tomorrow.
  • Last night: only when someone shares sleep data.
  • Space: the next launch with its T-minus, whether it’s visible from home, and the ISS when it passes high.
  • News: a couple of headlines, space news and one AI-made line, labelled as AI.
  • A picture of the day and, at the foot, plan limits.

The briefing also shows the alarm controls while the alarm is still ringing: Snooze, I’m up and Scrub.

How long it stays

An alarm briefing stays until you choose Done, or until the Briefing stays up time runs out: 1 h, 2 h (the default), 3 h or until noon, counted from the original wake. I’m up and reconnecting don’t extend it. Change it in Settings › Night & alarm on a wall or TV, or Night › Crew & snooze defaults on Apple.

Open it any time

Tap the Weather tile on the panel, the Today tile on a TV, or a weather alert. An on-demand briefing closes after 45 minutes on the panel and 30 minutes on a TV.
