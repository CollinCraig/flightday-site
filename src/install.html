Step two

Add your home (Home Assistant)

  • Home Assistant 2026.1 or later, on your own network.
  • HACS, to add Flight Day for Home Assistant, which runs the launch alarm, Goodnight and comfort for the whole house. You install it once.
  • A screen: a phone, a tablet, a Mac, a TV or a wall panel. Pair a TV or panel from your phone; there’s no keyboard to fight with.

Optional

A Flight Day Hub on a computer at home, a bedroom speaker, smart lights, a thermostat, a robot vacuum, cameras, room sensors and an AI model in Home Assistant. Each one lights up when it’s there. See Integrations.
