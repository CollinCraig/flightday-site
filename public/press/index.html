Description

One paragraph

I’m building Flight Day: a free, open-source alarm clock, for every screen you own. A launch-style countdown wakes you while the screen brightens, and at liftoff the morning briefing takes over with the weather, rain in the next hour, your calendar and the launches worth watching. At night, one tap on Goodnight dims the screens. Then it grows into your whole home dashboard and day planner as you add things: the thermostat and Smart Comfort, lights, speakers, a robot vacuum, a Systems check for what needs attention at home, and a live Space feed. Flight Day runs on an Android wall panel and Google TVs and works with Home Assistant. iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch apps are in private TestFlight testing; Mac and Apple TV apps are in development. The code is open source: github.com/CollinCraig/flightday.

Mark and icon

Daybreak Ascent

A half sun rising on a horizon, with an ascent trail that lifts off and pitches east toward a small craft: a launch at dawn, and the arc of a day. Keep one craft diameter of clear space around it, and don’t rotate, stretch, outline or add flames.
