        About these docs. They describe the current build: Web/Android 1.10.3 on a wall panel and Google TVs, and the iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch apps in private TestFlight testing. Mac and Apple TV apps are in development. Flight Day runs in one household today. Anything not built yet carries a label: In development or Concept.

        The install guide is being prepared. Until then, try the demo house: the real web app in your browser, with sample data and Sam and Riley as the crew. It never connects to a house.
