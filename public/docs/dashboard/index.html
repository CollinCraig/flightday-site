      Your whole day and home

      The dashboard and your day

      Home shows the day at a glance, in numbers rather than adjectives. Every tile shows real data, or says plainly what it’s waiting for.

      The Home tiles

      TileWhat it shows
      Clock and next upThe time, the date and the next thing: an event, the wake time or a launch with its T-minus. Always first.
      WeatherNow, a one-line summary and rain soon. Tap it on the panel for the briefing.
      TodayYour calendar. After 8 PM, or once the last event has ended, it leads with Tomorrow.
      Due nextCoursework deadlines, when connected.
      Sleep & WakeTomorrow’s wake time, the alarm and Goodnight.
      Scenes & lightsScenes and how many lights are on.
      Now playingOnly while something plays.
      AlertsOnly when there are any: weather alerts and house notices.
      Plan limitsHow much of your AI plans is left, when connected.

      A LIVE banner appears across the top while a launch is in flight, with a Watch button.

      Arrange Home

      1. Press and hold a Home tile, or press Menu on a TV remote.
      2. Show or hide each tile and move it up or down. The clock always stays first.
      3. Choose Done. Reset puts the default order back.

      Each screen keeps its own arrangement. On iPhone, iPad and Mac it’s in Settings › Screens › Home tiles; Apple TV keeps a fixed layout.

      Your calendar

      On the wall panel and TVs, Today comes from the calendars in your Home Assistant. The Apple apps read the calendar on the device (read-only, the next 7 days).

      Planning your day

      Flight Plan, an AI-made plan for the day from your calendar and routines, is a Concept.
