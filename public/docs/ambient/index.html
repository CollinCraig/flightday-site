      Your whole day and home

      Ambient

      Fully free at launch. In development, aiming for 8 Dec: web/Android and iPhone first. Apple TV, Mac and Watch are coming after launch. These docs also describe later platform work.

      When nobody’s looking, a screen shows the time, the date, the temperature, the next event and the wake time over a picture, with its credit.

      Still, Animated or Live

      • Still (the default): a picture from NASA’s libraries or the day’s launch.
      • Animated: the same picture drifting slowly. Reduce Motion stops the drift.
      • Live: a muted live stream when there is one, otherwise “no live stream right now · animated meanwhile”. Apple TV offers a LIVE · Watch button instead.

      Set it per screen in Settings › Ambient, or Settings › Screens.

      When it starts

      After 5 idle minutes on the wall panel and TVs (Settings › Ambient: never, or 1 to 30 minutes), 5 minutes on Apple TV and 10 minutes on an iPad on the wall. Near a featured launch, Flight Director takes over. At night and during the alarm, Ambient stays out of the way.

      Leave Ambient

      Touch the screen or press any key. On a TV, Play/Pause turns Ambient on and off.

      When something plays in another room, Ambient names only the source and the room, never the title.
