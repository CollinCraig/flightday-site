      Things to try

      Fully free at launch. In development, aiming for 8 Dec: web/Android and iPhone first. Apple TV, Mac and Watch are coming after launch. These docs also describe later platform work.

      Quick wins in today’s build. None of them touch the house.

      1. Preview the alarm. Night › Tonight › Try the alarm runs a 45-second countdown and liftoff on that screen only. It’s also in Settings › Display.
      2. See the night screen. Night › Try night screen, then touch the screen to bring up the dim controls.
      3. Pick your night look. In the night controls, switch This screen between Pure black and Night clock.
      4. Arrange Home. Press and hold a Home tile (or press Menu on a TV remote) to hide, show and reorder tiles.
      5. Follow a launch. In Space, pick a launch and choose Track, then Watch to open Flight Director.
      6. Change the picture. Switch Ambient between Still, Animated and Live in Settings › Ambient. On a TV, Play/Pause turns Ambient on and off.
      7. Show both clocks. Settings › Display › time format Both shows “07:45 (7:45 AM)”.
      8. Open the briefing any time. Tap the Weather tile on the panel, or the Today tile on a TV.
      9. Set how long the briefing stays. Settings › Night & alarm › Briefing stays up: 1 h, 2 h, 3 h or until noon.
      10. Make one morning different. Night › Tomorrow only changes one wake time and leaves the week alone.
