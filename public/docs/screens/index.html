      Reference

      What am I looking at?

      Fully free at launch. In development, aiming for 8 Dec: web/Android and iPhone first. Apple TV, Mac and Watch are coming after launch. These docs also describe later platform work.

      Flight Day shows real data or says plainly what it’s waiting for. A missing value is never a default, and an old value carries its age.

      The headline in each area

      AreaLook here first
      HomeThe big clock and the line under it: the next event, the wake time or a launch’s T-minus. Then Weather, and Today or Tomorrow.
      SpaceThe top row of the manifest, with its T-minus, and the brief under the globe.
      HouseThe lights that are on, what’s playing, and the thermostat’s mode and setpoint.
      NightTomorrow’s wake time, whether the alarm is armed, and Tonight: what Goodnight will do.
      StatusWhat’s connected and what isn’t, and plan limits.

      Fresh, old and missing

      You seeIt means
      “as of 6:10 AM” on a dimmed tileThe data is older than it should be. It’s the last value received, not a fresh reading.
      A standby line, such as “Thermostat not connected” or “Calendar not connected”That source isn’t connected. Nothing is filled in for it.
      “Not connected” in Status, with a listThe sources that have no connection, each with a link to the setting that fixes it.
      “Home Assistant · not connected — add URL + token in Settings”No house is set up on this screen.
      “This card could not load · try again from Settings → Reload”A tile failed to draw.
      “House action not confirmed · reconnecting”A command didn’t come back confirmed. Don’t assume it worked.

      The alarm

      You seeIt means
      “T-04:12 · liftoff 6:50 AM”The count to your wake time.
      “liftoff 6:50 AM · up by 7:20 AM · 1 snooze”The alarm header: liftoff, the up-by time and snoozes used so far.
      “Snoozed · liftoff 7:00 AM”, or “snoozing · liftoff …” at nightWaiting to count down again.
      “No more snoozes — up by …”The up-by time is too close for another snooze.
      “Final launch”The last liftoff before up-by. I’m up needs a hold.
      “muted · the lights will still rise”The bedroom speaker is muted. Flight Day never unmutes it in the morning.
      “house offline — lights not held”The screen is running its own backup alarm, without the house.
      “Up … · no answer”Nobody answered; the alarm stopped after 15 minutes.

      The house

      You seeIt means
      “Comfort · waiting for the house” or “Comfort offline”No comfort status has arrived from Home Assistant yet.
      “Room sensors · waiting for Apple Home”No room readings have arrived yet.
      “· not counted” on a dimmed roomThat reading is too old, or the room is left out, so comfort ignores it.
      “Comfort is quiet for the night”A bedroom screen at night: comfort controls wait until morning.
      “Choose Heat or Cool to see its setpoint”The thermostat is Off, so there’s no setpoint to show.
      “Needs a hand”The vacuum is stuck or needs maintenance.

      Labels and badges

      You seeIt means
      LIVE bannerA launch is in flight. Watch opens it.
      Plan limits: live, standby, “report from …”, “limit hit”, “est.”The state of each AI plan’s usage report. A limit the service doesn’t report is never made up.
      A yellow “demo” chipThat tile shows sample data, on purpose.
      “AI” on a lineThe line was made by AI.
      “Estimated · typical ascent, not telemetry”Flight Director’s altitude and speed are a typical ascent, not live data.

      Coming later

      Go / No-Go In development (in the demo house; arrives with the next app release): a line at the top of the briefing, for example “Checks incomplete · weather as of 6:10 AM”. Until then, the briefing shows each input with its own age.
