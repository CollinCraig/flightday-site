      Your whole day and home

      Systems check and the AI planner

      AI helps around the edges: a line in the briefing today, and more as it’s ready. Every AI-made line says so.

      Built today

      • AI line in the briefing In development: one AI-made news line, labelled as AI, with “more ›” for the rest.
      • Plan limits In development: how much of your AI plans is left this week and when each resets, on Home and in Status. A limit the service doesn’t report is never made up.

      Systems check

      In development What needs attention at home: batteries, offline devices, automation errors. Each finding is a Caution or a Warning. When there’s nothing, it says “Nothing needs you”, and “not run yet today” before its first run. Integrations report findings too (see Build your own).

      AI comfort plan

      In development A plan for the day’s temperatures made from the forecast and the rooms, checked by the house rules before anything acts. See Smart Comfort.

      Flight Plan

      Concept An AI-made plan for your day from your calendar and routines.

      Where AI requests go

      Flight Day has no AI service of its own. When released, AI features go through the AI provider you choose in your Home Assistant. See Privacy.
