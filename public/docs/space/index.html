      Your whole day and home

      The Space feed

      Fully free at launch. In development, aiming for 8 Dec: web/Android and iPhone first. Apple TV, Mac and Watch are coming after launch. These docs also describe later platform work.

      Every launch on a live globe, from public launch data. It’s on by default and needs no setup; turn the area off per screen if you don’t want it.

      OptionalIn development

      Launches and the globe

      1. Open Space and pick a launch from the manifest. The globe flies to its pad.
      2. The brief below shows the vehicle, pad and T-minus, and “Visible from home: look …” when that can be worked out from your location.
      3. Choose Watch. Before the stream is live, Flight Director shows the count, the pad and the published timeline.
      4. Choose Track to keep a mission selected on every screen. Settings › Stop tracking lets it go.

      Status comes from the launch itself. A late launch time isn’t proof that a rocket is flying.

      The ISS and LOOK UP

      The globe shows the station’s past and predicted track, launch pads and home, with a flat map if the globe can’t run. The pass box says “Next pass … · max 54° · 11 min”. During a visible pass, a LOOK UP card points to the station on Ambient and the night controls.

      Flight Director

      For the first nine minutes after a confirmed liftoff it shows altitude, speed and pitch labelled “Estimated · typical ascent, not telemetry”. Pad weather appears only when a real forecast covers the launch time.
