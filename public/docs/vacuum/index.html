      Your whole day and home

      Robot vacuum

      Status, battery and the map, with Start, Pause and Return to dock. It appears when your Home Assistant has a vacuum. Roborock gets room cleaning and maintenance through its own adapter.

      OptionalIn development

      The card

      • The status and battery (“82% battery”), and the latest map.
      • Start, Pause and Return to dock act on the real vacuum.
      • Home shows only “Cleaning…” or “Stuck · …”, and a “Needs a hand” chip when it needs you.

      Cleaning one room

      In development Clean a room lists the rooms the vacuum reports. Rooms with the same name show their number. It has been tested against a simulated vacuum so far.

      Maintenance

      The main brush, side brush, filter, sensors and strainer, each with the time left or “Overdue”. An item at or below zero is overdue. Nothing resets by itself.
