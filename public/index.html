A better alarm clock, on every screen you own.

Your alarm clock and day, in one place. A launch countdown wakes you while the screen brightens, the morning briefing follows at liftoff, and Goodnight dims the screens at night. Built for a wall panel and Google TVs, with Apple apps in development.

The alarm clock

Wake up, wind down, and the day in between

Every screen counts down to your wake time, then hands you the day. At night, one tap puts everything to bed. Five areas, the same on every device: Home · Space · House · Night · Status.

Launch alarm

A ten-minute countdown while the screen brightens, liftoff at your wake time, then the briefing. Smart lights, if you have them, rise too, in their warmest colour.

T-04:12 · liftoff 6:50 AM

Snooze and Back to bed

Snooze 10 min, I’m up, and Scrub as a deliberate hold, so a sleepy tap can’t cancel the morning. A “must be up by” limit caps the snoozing.

liftoff 7:00 AM · up by 7:20 AM

Morning briefing

The weather and rain in the next hour, your day, what’s due and the next launch, in numbers rather than adjectives. It stays until you choose Done.

Rain from 8:10 AM, light · first event 9:30 AM

Goodnight

One tap dims the screens for the night. Wake a screen and it answers in dim red on black, with labels and values only.

Wake 7:00 AM · alarm armed

Sleep schedule

Wake times by day, a separate change for tomorrow only, and a bedtime target that never starts anything by itself.

Mon–Fri 7:00 AM · Sat–Sun 8:30 AM

Clock, calendar and weather

The time, the next event, Today or Tomorrow, and a free forecast from Open-Meteo, on every screen.

54° · cloudy · next: Team standup 9:00 AM

Devices

Same features, each device’s own way

The goal is feature parity, not identical pixels. The wall and the TVs share one web app; the Apple apps are native, with SF type, widgets and Siri Remote focus.

Wall panel In use

An Android wall panel, touch first. Flight Day is its home screen and controls its backlight at night.

Google TV In use

Remote and D-pad navigation. A TV can be a night screen and play the alarm.

iPhone Private testing

Alarm notifications with Snooze, I’m up and Scrub, Lock Screen widgets and Live Activities.

iPad Private testing

The full dashboard in portrait or landscape, and a wall-mounted night mode.

Mac In development

A sidebar dashboard and a menu-bar countdown to the next liftoff.

Apple TV In development

Glanceable Home and Space, the alarm with Siri Remote controls, Top Shelf.

Apple Watch Private testing

The alarm on your wrist, complications and an offline “as of” mode. Double tap to snooze is in development.
